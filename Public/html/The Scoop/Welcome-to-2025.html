Cheers to a Sweet 2025! 🍒 Start the Year with Cherries On Top Ice Cream

By Maxine Finney | 01.15.2025

Cherries On Top Ice Cream – Welcome to 2025! Happy New Year from Cherries On Top Ice Cream! As we ring in 2025, we’re excited to be part of your upcoming celebrations. Do you have an event or party planned this year? Whether it’s a wedding, birthday, corporate event, or special gathering, we’d love to make it truly memorable. At Cherries On Top, we bring the "cherry on top" to your event. Our crew is the cherry on top of your occasion, serving up delicious hand-scooped sundaes, creamy milkshakes, and vintage soda floats that will transport your guests to a time of classic indulgence. From first bites to last sips, we promise to deliver sweet moments everyone will talk about. Tailored to You: Dietary Accommodations for All We know that everyone has different dietary needs, and we want every guest to enjoy our treats! Whether you're looking for dairy-free, gluten-free, or other specialty options, we’ve got you covered. Our team works hard to accommodate all dietary restrictions, so no one has to miss out on the fun. You can rest easy knowing there’s something sweet for everyone! Book Your Event Today – Dates Fill Fast! Our calendar for 2025 is already filling up quickly, so don’t wait too long to reserve your event date with us. We’d love to bring our vintage-inspired ice cream truck or catering service to your next celebration and serve up smiles all around. Let Cherries On Top Ice Cream make your event the highlight of the year. Reach out today to secure your spot, and let’s create something truly special together!

Cheers to a sweet 2025! 🍒✨
