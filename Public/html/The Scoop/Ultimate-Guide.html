Sweeten Your Celebration with Cherries On Top Catering: The Ultimate Guide to Ice Cream Truck Delights

Planning a party or celebration that everyone will remember fondly? Look no further than Cherries On Top Catering and our delightful ice cream truck offerings! Whether you're hosting a birthday bash, corporate event, wedding, or any special occasion, our menu is designed to add a sweet touch that will leave your guests smiling. Classic Sundaes: Timeless Treats Imagine your guests indulging in scoops of creamy, hard-scooped ice cream topped with their favorite sauces, nuts, whipped cream, and of course, a cherry on top! Our classic sundaes are a crowd-pleaser that never goes out of style. Fruit Smoothies: Fresh and FlavorfulFor those looking for a refreshing option, our fruit smoothies are a hit. Blended with fresh fruits and creamy yogurt or sorbet, they provide a healthy and delicious alternative that's perfect for warm summer days or any time of year. Old-Fashioned Soda Floats: Nostalgic Nectar Transport your guests back in time with our old-fashioned soda floats. Choose from a variety of sodas paired with scoops of ice cream for a fizzy, creamy concoction that evokes nostalgia and delight in every sip. Gelatti Creations: Italian-Inspired Elegance Indulge in the richness of our gelatti creations, inspired by the flavors of summer. From traditional favorites like mango or tropical to innovative blends, our stacked gelatti will satisfy even the most discerning palate.
